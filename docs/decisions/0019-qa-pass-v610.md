# 0019. The full QA pass before the architecture phase (V6.10)

- Status: accepted (2026-11-18)
- Builds on: ADR 0015 (consoles and access), 0017 (assessment), 0018 (home and header)

## Context

The owner asked for a complete check of the prototype before the code is reorganised for a back end and the
agent: every kind of account, what each one sees, every button, entering and leaving each part, the browser's
back button, retrying, and whether data survives signing out. Logical faults were to be mended without asking;
choices that are his were to be listed together at the end. Three changes came with the request: no emergency
room in the header, a clearer name for the ER card's action, and a larger coach card with a photograph.

## How it was checked

Headless Chrome at 1440 x 900 and 390 x 844, with fresh storage, as eight accounts: an independent nursing
trainee and an independent medical one (both by registration), a university nursing student and a university
medical student (by roster e-mail), a supervisor, a coordinator, a university's admin and Nabd's admin. For
each: the header, the sidebar, the home's sections, the account menu, and where navigation to every view leads.
Then a student's flows end to end: register, play, leave mid-case, come back, finish, retry, sign out, sign in,
reload; every profile tab, every sidebar and menu item; the assignments; the consoles' pages.

## What was sound

- Data survives sign-out, sign-in and reload (name, track, sessions, reports).
- An independent trainee sees no academic part; a university student sees it; a refused e-mail is refused with
  the reason.
- The track decides the role in the room (nursing: nurse; medicine: doctor).
- Leaving the room mid-case keeps the case and stops its clock; "repeat the case" and "next case" work.
- Every console page renders for its role; sign-out works everywhere.

## What was wrong, and the fix

| Fault | Fix |
|---|---|
| The browser's back button left or reloaded the site from every view but the room. | Each view change is a history entry (`admin/060-shell.js`); back returns to the view before. The room keeps its own handling. |
| A console account could open the legacy university portal, the profile and the last-session view. | They lead back to the console. |
| An assignment (and the coach's case buttons) did not open its case while another case sat unstarted at its briefing; the room said "you have a case under way". | `erplus/040-open-case.js`: an unstarted or finished case is replaced; one under way is kept and the trainee is told. |
| "تقرير آخر جلسة" said "no report" to a trainee with saved reports. | It opens his last saved report. |
| The profile opened on two notices above the trainee's own card. | His card first, then the affiliation and the reports. |
| The profile's coach card listed four modules of the old coach; "skills by section" stayed empty; "repeated errors" stayed empty. | The cards show what the coach page says: what he needs to develop, his ER axes, his repeated errors. |
| An independent trainee saw a "supervisor's notes" card. | University students only, and it shows the supervisor's note. |
| The settings tab held one button. | "بياناتي": name, track, year and photograph (shrunk to 160 px, kept on the device). A university student's are fixed by the roster and the course. |
| The record's serial read `NABD-2026-LOCAL_-101`. | Made from the e-mail. |
| Registering with an e-mail that has an account overwrote its name and track. | It signs in to the account and says so. |
| A university student's year was always 4. | From the course number (MED-512: fifth year). |
| The sidebar read «الـ ER»; a locked room was tagged «جديد». | Arabic labels; «قريباً». |
| The university block's "open the portal" button scrolled to itself; the hero's button said "portal". | The button is gone; the hero's says «واجباتي الجامعية». |
| The footer had two emergency-room links. | One. |
| The level quiz stayed open over other views. | It closes when the view changes. |

Also in this version: the header without the emergency room (the button stays in the page, not shown, for the
scripts that press it); «ابدأ مناوبتك في الطوارئ» on the ER card; the coach's card on the home, larger, with a
photograph and three points.

## Left for the owner (listed in the reply of 2026-11-18)

Signing in for the first time without registering; switching role in the room; asking the year at
registration; the two locked rooms in the sidebar; the legacy level quiz.
